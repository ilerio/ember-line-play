Ember Line

How to play

  1. Day. Walk out to the ruins and hold E to search. Each search costs an hour of daylight, and ruins can't be searched at night.
  2. Spend scrap. Recruit wanderers, give them roles at the stations, and raise walls in the six slots.
  3. Night. The dead come from both sides. Aim with the mouse, click to shoot, and aim high for headshots.
  4. Win. Survive to dawn after night 10. Every 5th night is a blood moon.
